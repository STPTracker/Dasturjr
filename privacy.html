Privacy & data use

How VeriTap collects, stores and uses information. ← Back to scanner

What is collected

Student identity details entered at registration (name, ID, roll number, class, division, course, and an optional photo, phone, email and guardian contact); an NFC card identifier; WebAuthn credential metadata; and attendance timestamps.

What is never collected

VeriTap never accesses, downloads, stores or reconstructs a raw fingerprint image or biometric template. Biometric matching happens entirely inside the student's phone using its secure hardware. The only thing that reaches this system is a cryptographic yes/no verification result and, at registration time, a public key — never anything that could be used to recreate a fingerprint or face scan.

Why it's collected

To identify students accurately, prevent proxy (impersonated) attendance, and give administrators and teachers an auditable, correctable attendance record.

Where it's stored

In your institution's own Firebase project (Firestore for records, Firebase Storage for photos), governed by the security rules in firestore.rules. Nothing is sent to any third party beyond Google Cloud/Firebase infrastructure that hosts the database itself.

Who can access it

Access is role-based: Super Admins and Admins can view and manage student and attendance records; Teachers can view and manage attendance for their own assigned classes only. Security logs are visible only to Admin-level accounts and cannot be edited by anyone.

Data retention

Retention periods are configurable by a Super Admin in Settings. Institutions should set a retention period appropriate to their own record-keeping obligations and applicable data-protection requirements, and should obtain appropriate institutional and parental/student authorization before enrolling real biometric verification for any student, particularly minors.

Your rights

Students and guardians may request a copy of, correction to, or deletion of the student's stored records by contacting the institution's administrator directly.
